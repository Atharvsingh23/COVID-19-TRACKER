How Contagious is Corona virus ??

The coronavirus is highly contagious and spreads primarily through respiratory droplets when an infected person coughs, sneezes, or talks. It can also spread by touching contaminated surfaces and then touching your face. Proper precautions like wearing masks, maintaining social distance, and frequent handwashing are essential to reduce its spread.

  • Human Contact

    The coronavirus can spread through direct human contact, such as shaking hands or touching an infected person. Avoid close contact and practice social distancing to reduce the risk of transmission.

  • Through Air

    The coronavirus can spread through air transmission, primarily via respiratory droplets when an infected person coughs, sneezes, or talks. Proper ventilation and wearing masks can help reduce the risk.

  • Contaminated Objects

    The coronavirus can spread through contaminated objects when an individual touches a surface or object that has the virus on it and then touches their face, especially the mouth, nose, or eyes. Regular cleaning and disinfection of surfaces can help reduce the risk.

Corona Virus Symptoms

The symptoms of COVID-19 can range from mild to severe and may appear 2-14 days after exposure to the virus. Understanding these symptoms is crucial for early detection and prevention of further spread.

  • Coughing And Sneezing

    Persistent coughing and sneezing are common symptoms caused by irritation in the respiratory tract. These actions help expel mucus and irritants but can also spread the virus to others.

  • Hot Fever

    Fever is a natural response of the immune system to fight off infections. It indicates that the body is working to eliminate the virus by raising its temperature.

  • Strong Headache

    Headaches can result from inflammation or dehydration caused by the virus. They are often accompanied by other symptoms like fatigue and fever.

  • Shortness Of Breath

    Difficulty in breathing occurs when the virus affects the lungs, leading to inflammation and reduced oxygen exchange. This symptom requires immediate medical attention.

  • Confusion

    Confusion or mental disorientation can occur due to low oxygen levels in the blood or as a result of the body's immune response to the virus.

  • Sore Throat

    A sore throat is caused by inflammation of the throat tissues due to the virus. It is often accompanied by difficulty swallowing and irritation.

Preventing the Spread of Coronavirus

Effective prevention of COVID-19 requires a combination of personal responsibility, hygiene practices, and community cooperation. By following these measures, we can collectively reduce the spread of the virus and protect vulnerable populations.

  • Wash Your Hands Thoroughly

    Regularly wash your hands with soap and water for at least 20 seconds. This simple yet effective step removes germs and prevents the virus from spreading through touch.

  • Cover Your Nose and Mouth

    Always cover your nose and mouth with a tissue or your elbow when sneezing or coughing. Dispose of used tissues immediately and wash your hands to prevent the spread of respiratory droplets.

  • Wear a Mask

    Wearing a mask in public spaces, especially where social distancing is difficult, helps reduce the transmission of the virus. Ensure the mask covers both your nose and mouth for maximum effectiveness.

How to Wash Your Hands

Proper handwashing is one of the most effective ways to prevent the spread of germs and viruses. Follow these steps to ensure your hands are thoroughly clean.

  1. 1

    Wet Hands

    Start by wetting your hands with clean, running water.

  2. 2

    Apply Soap

    Apply enough soap to cover all surfaces of your hands.

  3. 3

    Rub Hands Palm to Palm

    Rub your palms together to create a lather and clean thoroughly.

  4. 4

    Lather The Back of Both Hands

    Lather the back of your hands, ensuring all areas are covered.

  5. 5

    Scrub Between Your Fingers

    Interlace your fingers and scrub between them to remove germs.

  6. 6

    Rub The Back of Fingers on The Opposing Palm

    Rub the back of your fingers against the opposing palm for a deeper clean.

  7. 7

    Clean Thumbs

    Grip and rotate each thumb to clean thoroughly.

  8. 8

    Wash Fingernails And Fingertips

    Scrub your fingernails and fingertips against your palm to remove dirt.

  9. 9

    Rinse Hands

    Rinse your hands thoroughly under clean, running water.

  10. 10

    Dry with a Single Use Towel

    Use a clean, single-use towel to dry your hands completely.

  11. 11

    Use The Towel to Turn off The Faucet

    Turn off the faucet using the towel to avoid recontamination.

  12. 12

    Your Hands Are Clean

    Congratulations! Your hands are now clean and germ-free.
